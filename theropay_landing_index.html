The real problem

You built the product. Then the bank account fell through.

For crypto and fintech businesses, banking is the bottleneck — not the idea. Here's what actually slows global growth, and why it compounds.

01The 9-month wait

Traditional banks take 6–9 months to approve crypto and fintech businesses — opaque reviews, endless documentation, no clear timeline. Deals and runway don't wait that long.

02Declined without a reason

Many applications are routed into enhanced due diligence and quietly declined — often with no explanation. Worse: repeated rejections can flag you in banks' internal risk systems, making the next bank even harder.

03De-banked overnight

One sponsor bank is a single point of failure. Accounts get closed without notice the moment "crypto" enters the conversation — and your payroll, payouts, and treasury stop with them.

04A stack held together by tape

Separate vendors for banking, payouts, FX, and compliance mean more reconciliation, more contracts, more cost. International wires can take up to a week; SWIFT corridors quietly add fees on top.
